For the PP, the icoprog convention is that PPDIR is low to read from FPGA, high to write. If PPDIR is low, the FPGA should drive the PP data bits. Pulsing PPCLK high acknowledges receipt of the data by the Pi. To transfer from he Pi to the FPGA, first drive PPDIR high, drive the data bits, then pulse PPCLK high to indicate data is available.
